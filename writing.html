Julie Tedjeske Crane

Law librarian. Legal research professor.

Julie shares the materials from her course AI and Legal Research on this site so that law librarians and legal educators can adapt the materials for their own teaching. The materials are free to use for noncommercial educational purposes under a Creative Commons license.

Writing

Julie has written 26 posts for the RIPS Law Librarian Blog since 2021, and she has written 25 posts since 2022 for Keeping It Brief, the blog of the Dickinson Law library. In 2021 and 2022, she wrote 2 posts for the blog of AALL’s Legal Innovation and Technology Special Interest Section (LIT-SIS). From 2021 to 2025, she also wrote 15 columns, book reviews, and program reviews for the ALL-SIS Newsletter of AALL’s Academic Law Libraries Special Interest Section.
