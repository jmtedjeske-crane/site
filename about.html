About

Julie Tedjeske Crane is a reference librarian and Professor of Legal Research at the H. Laddie Montague Jr. Law Library at Penn State Dickinson Law. She teaches first-year and upper-level legal research courses, and she manages the library’s LibGuides, which serve as most of the library’s website.

Julie practiced civil litigation from 2000 to 2017 at Reed Smith, Duane Morris, and Blank Rome, with an emphasis in construction law. She returned to librarianship in 2017, and she has worked in academic law libraries since then.

Julie’s scholarship examines how law teachers can design legal research courses, assignments, and grading so that students learn to evaluate sources, including output from generative AI tools. Her recent work addresses grading legal research and sustainable work practices for librarians.

Julie serves as Vice-Chair/Chair-Elect of the Research Instruction and Patron Services Special Interest Section (RIPS-SIS) of the American Association of Law Libraries (AALL), and she serves on the AALL Spectrum Editorial Board.

This site is Julie’s personal project. It is not an official publication of Penn State Dickinson Law.

Law Librarian and Professor of Legal Research

Penn State Dickinson Law · July 2022 to present

Julie teaches first-year and upper-level legal research courses, and she gives guest lectures on request. She provides ready-reference and in-depth research services. Since 2025, she has managed the library’s LibGuides, which serve as most of the library’s website, and she writes posts for Keeping It Brief, the library’s blog. From 2022 to 2025, she managed the law school’s Digital Commons repository and the faculty’s Selected Works profiles.

Faculty Scholarship and Educational Technology Librarian

Chicago-Kent College of Law · July 2021 to June 2022

Julie supervised three full-time faculty support specialists. She managed the law school’s Digital Commons repository, the faculty’s Selected Works profiles, and the faculty’s SSRN postings, and she worked with student editors to maintain the school’s journals in Digital Commons. She also taught training sessions on Clio, Microsoft Word, and other tools.

Electronic Resources and Instructional Services Librarian

Duquesne University · February 2017 to June 2021

Julie built a new WordPress website for the Allegheny County Law Library, which had previously been part of the law school’s website. She administered the library’s Springshare platforms (LibGuides, LibAnswers, and LibCal), and she wrote nearly all of the library’s research guides, blog posts, and FAQ entries. She also built a system, first in Qualtrics and later in LibAnswers, that tracked reference transactions and librarian presentations.

Litigation Attorney

Blank Rome LLP, 2015–2017 · Duane Morris LLP, 2004–2015 · Reed Smith LLP, 2000–2004

Julie represented clients in general business and civil litigation in federal and state courts, with an emphasis in construction law. She managed electronic discovery, conducted legal research, drafted pleadings, motions, briefs, and discovery requests, and argued motions. She also handled extensive pro bono work.

Earlier Library Positions

University of Pittsburgh, 1995–1997 · La Roche College, 1993–1994

As a Reference and Technical Services Librarian at the University of Pittsburgh, Julie cataloged the collection of the Western Psychiatric Institute and Clinic’s library, and she provided research help to faculty, staff, and students. As the Technical Services Librarian at La Roche College, she cataloged the library’s collection and supervised library operations on weekends and one evening each week.

Teaching
